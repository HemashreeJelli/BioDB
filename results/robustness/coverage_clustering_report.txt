  The binary metric counts a variant as mismatched whenever the single
  highest-frequency population was not studied, even if a studied
  population carries a near-identical frequency. The coverage ratio
  measures how much of the peak frequency the studied cohorts actually
  reach, so near-ties no longer register as neglect.
